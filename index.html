About Me

I'm a full stack developer at Ford, and I take freelance web work on the side. Most of that has been Next.js sites for small businesses, including a booking site for a bridal beauty company and a gallery site for a landscape design firm.

I'm back in school at Oakland Community College, working through prerequisites before starting a Master's in computer science. I want to focus on machine learning and AI, which is a long way from the CSS I'm writing this week, but you have to start somewhere.

Outside of work I spend most of my free time on my boat. It's either very relaxing or a running list of repairs, depending on the week.
